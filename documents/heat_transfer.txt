COURSE: MEEG-403 Heat Transfer
CREDITS: 3

MEEG-403 Heat Transfer covers analytical and numerical methods used to study heat transfer.

The course addresses the three major modes of heat transfer: conduction, convection, and radiation.

Students study both steady-state and unsteady heat flow. Additional topics include heat transfer involving condensing vapors and boiling liquids.

Heat Transfer is part of the thermal-fluid science area of the Mechanical Engineering curriculum and is normally taken during the senior year.

The March 2024 Mechanical Engineering handbook lists Applied Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics II, and Computer Aided Engineering as prerequisites for MEEG-403.
