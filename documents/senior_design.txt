COURSES: MEEG-441 Senior Project I and MEEG-442 Senior Project II
CREDITS: 3 credits each

Howard Mechanical Engineering's senior project sequence consists of MEEG-441 Senior Project I and MEEG-442 Senior Project II.

MEEG-441 is an independent team design study completed under the supervision of Mechanical Engineering faculty members. Students work on an engineering design problem of current interest.

The senior project includes a formal reporting component. A project report must be submitted for review by Mechanical Engineering faculty before graduation.

MEEG-442 continues the team design study begun in MEEG-441.

This makes Senior Project a two-course sequence rather than an isolated one-semester project. Students progress from Senior Project I into Senior Project II while continuing their team engineering design work.

The Mechanical Engineering curriculum places Senior Project I in the first senior semester and Senior Project II in the second senior semester.
