COURSE: MEEG-204 Solid Mechanics
CREDITS: 3

MEEG-204 Solid Mechanics introduces the behavior of deformable solid bodies under mechanical loading.

Major topics include the basic concepts of stress and strain, states of stress and strain, Mohr's circle, and bending of prismatic bars.

The course provides students with a foundation for analyzing how engineering components deform and carry loads. These concepts are important for later courses involving mechanical design, structures, manufacturing, and component failure.

Within the Howard Mechanical Engineering curriculum, Solid Mechanics is taken during the second semester of the sophomore year.

Howard also offers MEEG-206 Solid Mechanics Lab, a one-credit laboratory course. The laboratory exposes students to experimental methods used to evaluate mechanical properties, including measurement of static and dynamic properties.
