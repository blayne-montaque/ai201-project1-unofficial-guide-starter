COURSE: MEEG-304 Thermodynamics
CREDITS: 3

MEEG-304 Thermodynamics introduces the basic concepts and definitions used in thermodynamics. The course covers the laws of thermodynamics, properties of pure substances, entropy, and availability.

Within the Howard University Mechanical Engineering curriculum, Thermodynamics is a junior-level course. It appears in the first semester of the junior year and carries three credits.

The course serves as part of the thermal sciences foundation of the Mechanical Engineering curriculum. Topics such as thermodynamic properties, energy relationships, entropy, and availability prepare students for later thermal-fluid courses including Applied Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer.

According to the Mechanical Engineering curriculum, students take Thermodynamics alongside courses such as Fluid Mechanics, Mechanical Design I, Manufacturing, and Probability and Statistics during the first semester of junior year.
