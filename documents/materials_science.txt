COURSE: MEEG-209 Materials Science
CREDITS: 3

MEEG-209 Materials Science studies the relationship between the internal structure of engineering materials and their mechanical and physical properties.

The course includes metals, ceramics, and organic materials.

A major idea in the course is that material properties can be controlled or changed by modifying the material's microstructure.

The course also considers the stability and behavior of materials in service environments.

Materials Science provides a foundation for later engineering courses involving mechanical design, manufacturing, structural behavior, and material selection.
