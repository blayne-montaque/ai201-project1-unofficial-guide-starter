COURSE: MEEG-301 System Dynamics
CREDITS: 3

MEEG-301 System Dynamics introduces students to modern control theory.

The course includes digital control, nonlinear system analysis, and mathematical modeling of physical systems.

Systems modeled in the course may contain mechanical, fluid, thermal, and electrical elements.

The course also introduces classical feedback control theory and control-system design. These concepts are applied to practical electromechanical and aerospace engineering problems.

System Dynamics therefore connects mathematical modeling with the behavior and control of real engineering systems.

Within the Howard Mechanical Engineering curriculum, the course is taken during the second semester of the junior year.
