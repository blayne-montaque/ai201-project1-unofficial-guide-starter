TOPIC: Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering

Howard University's Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering program includes coursework in basic sciences, mathematics, and engineering sciences.

The program emphasizes applying engineering fundamentals to analysis, design, and experimentation.

Students can pursue technical coursework in several Mechanical Engineering subdisciplines, including applied mechanics, energy systems, manufacturing engineering, and aerospace engineering.

Applied mechanics focuses on forces, motion, and the design and development of systems and components that transmit motion, forces, or power.

Energy systems involve systems that generate and transmit power, including reciprocating and rotating engines.

Manufacturing engineering focuses on planning, designing, and arranging manufacturing processes and equipment. Computer-aided design and manufacturing are important parts of this area.

Aerospace engineering deals with the motion and engineering of systems such as airplanes, missiles, satellites, and rockets.

The Mechanical Engineering program is accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET.
