COURSE: MEEG-418 Vibration Analysis
CREDITS: 3
CATEGORY: Technical Elective - Applied Mechanics/Aerospace

MEEG-418 Vibration Analysis studies the dynamic behavior of vibrating mechanical systems.

The course covers single-degree-of-freedom, two-degree-of-freedom, and multi-degree-of-freedom vibration systems.

Students also study mode shape functions and numerical methods for solving vibration problems.

Software packages are incorporated into vibration analysis, connecting the mathematical theory of vibration with computational engineering methods.

Within Howard's Mechanical Engineering program, Vibration Analysis is listed as a technical elective in the Applied Mechanics/Aerospace area.
