COURSE: MEEG-316 Instrumentation & Experimentation Lab
CREDITS: 2

MEEG-316 Instrumentation and Experimentation Lab covers the basic principles of instruments and sensors used in engineering measurements.

The course also addresses experimental error and uncertainty analysis.

This course is part of the experimental component of the Mechanical Engineering curriculum and introduces students to the process of obtaining, interpreting, and evaluating engineering measurement data.

The Howard curriculum places Instrumentation and Experimentation during the second semester of the junior year.

Because engineering measurements always contain some degree of uncertainty, the course emphasizes understanding the quality and limitations of experimental measurements in addition to simply collecting data.
